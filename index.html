How We Make Our Caramel Apples

Follow our recipe to make a tasty caramel apple.

Ingredients

  • 6 firm, medium apples
  • 1 cup chopped nuts (walnuts or pecans)
  • Lemon juice and salt water solution (to prevent browning)

What You Need

6 sturdy wooden sticks, a sharp paring knife or carving tool, and a melon baller or spoon.

Directions

  1. Prep and de-wax the apples.
  2. Prepare a tray lined with parchment paper.
  3. Melt the caramels until smooth.
  4. Dip each apple and rotate to coat evenly.
  5. Add toppings while caramel is soft.
  6. Let the caramel set completely.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
